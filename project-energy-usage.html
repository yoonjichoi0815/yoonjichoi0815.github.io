Energy Usage & Weather Data Analysis

Term: March 2024 – April 2024 (CSE351 Project)

Tools: Python (Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib), Jupyter Notebook

🎯 Objective

Analyze how energy usage patterns vary with weather data using time-series techniques and exploratory analysis.

🛠️ Process Overview

  • Preprocessed and merged large-scale energy consumption and weather datasets
  • Applied time-series transformations and daily aggregations
  • Engineered features (e.g., temperature, humidity, consumption trends) for exploratory analysis

📊 Key Analysis

  • Created pipelines to clean and transform both weather and energy datasets
  • Calculated correlations between variables (e.g., temperature ↔ energy usage)
  • Visualized trends across different days and weather conditions using line plots and histograms

💡 Insights

  • Discovered seasonal and daily patterns in energy usage aligned with weather fluctuations
  • Learned the importance of pipeline structuring and temporal alignment in time-series datasets

🙋‍♀️ My Contribution

  • Built the entire notebook from scratch for time-series cleaning and visualization
  • Practiced modular coding and design of reproducible analysis workflow
